# Treatment lab dressing: third-party assets

Everything in this folder is **CC0 1.0 (public domain)**. No attribution is legally required; it is recorded here so the
sources stay traceable.

- Poly Haven: every asset on the site is CC0 (https://polyhaven.com/license).
- ambientCG: every asset on the site is CC0 (https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/).

Models were downloaded as glTF with 1K textures. They were then decimated, joined into one mesh and re-exported as glTF
in Blender 5.2. Some models got an extra `_LOD1` mesh. The textures are unchanged apart from one exception: the
`SackFlat` sack keeps only its geometry, because its "CEMENT" print was removed. The builder that places them is
`Assets/_Project/Scripts/Editor/TreatmentLabDressing.Real.cs` (menu `BTP/Setup/Furnish Treatment Lab`).

## Project assets reused (not third-party)

The young potted plants are the project's own crop models (`Assets/_Project/Prefabs/Plants/*_Farm.prefab`, using their
mid LOD mesh). Healthy plants use copies of their materials with the disease turned off
(`Assets/_Project/Materials/Treatment/Dressing/Plants`).
